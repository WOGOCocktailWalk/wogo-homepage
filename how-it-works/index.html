    How a WOGO Cocktail Walk Works

    No guide, no group — just three reserved bars, three cocktails and a digital route map in your inbox. Here's the whole night, step by step. From €29,95 per person.

    Step by step

    Your night in five steps

    From booking to the last cocktail — this is how a self-guided cocktail walk works.

    1

    🗓️Pick your city, route and date

    Book online in a couple of minutes. As soon as you book, we arrange your table at all three bars for that same day — the bar staff know WOGO guests are coming. Walks run from €29,95 per person; with 7 or more, book as a group and we can even personalise your route.

    2

    📩Get your map

    Your digital Cocktail Walk Map Guide lands in your inbox right after booking, with your route from bar 1 to bar 2 to bar 3. No app to install — it just works on your phone. (Can't find it? Check your spam folder first, then email us.)

    3

    🤫The bars are a surprise

    Which three bars? That stays secret until you've booked — it's half the fun. We post sneak peeks on Instagram, and the full line-up is revealed in your map right after payment. Expect hand-picked spots you'd probably never have found on your own.

    4

    🚶Walk it your way

    Only your start time at bar 1 is fixed — after that, the night is yours. Stay as long as you like at each stop; the bars are just 5–15 minutes' walk apart. You follow the route in order (bar 1 → 2 → 3), and most guests take 3–4 relaxed hours.

    5

    🍸At each bar

    Walk in, show your booking and give your full name to the bartender — your table is waiting. At every bar you choose from a special WOGO menu: one of two signature cocktails, or the mocktail. Most bars also serve snacks if you get peckish.

    The format, defined

    What is a self-guided cocktail walk?

    A cocktail walk is a bar crawl, upgraded: instead of queueing at random bars, you follow a curated route past three hand-picked cocktail bars, with a table pre-arranged and a signature cocktail included at every stop. Self-guided means exactly that — no guide, no tour group, no name badges. Just you and your own people, a route map on your phone, and a city to wander through.

    So how does a cocktail walk work in practice? You book, your tables are reserved, your route arrives by email, and you walk from bar 1 to bar 3 at your own pace. That's the whole idea — a self-guided bar crawl where all the planning is already done, and the only thing you can't skip is enjoying it.

    What's in your ticket

    ✓ Included

    • 3 hand-picked cocktail bars with your table reserved at each one
    • 3 drinks — at every bar you pick one of two signature cocktails, or the mocktail
    • Your digital Cocktail Walk Map Guide, sent by email right after booking
    • A special WOGO menu at each bar — staff briefed and expecting you

    ✕ Not included (so no surprises)

    • Transport between bars — it's a walk! Stops are 5–15 minutes apart on foot
    • Food — most bars serve snacks you can order, but dinner isn't part of the ticket
    • Extra drinks beyond your 3 — anything else you order, you settle with the bar

    We'd rather tell you now than surprise you at the bar. One ticket = one person's full walk.

    Good to know

    🥂

    Not drinking? Walk the Mocktail Walk

    Every route can be booked as a full Mocktail Walk — same bars, same reserved tables, an alcohol-free mocktail at every stop. Nobody has to sit the night out.

    🧭

    The route order is fixed

    Your bars are reserved in order, so the route always runs bar 1 → 2 → 3 and can't be shuffled. The exception: personalised routes for group bookings, which we plan around you.

    🛟

    A bar unexpectedly closed?

    It's rare, but it happens. Contact the team at info@wogoamsterdam.com or +31 20 210 1168 and we'll sort an alternative bar for you.
