Good to know

What's included?
Three hand-picked bars, a cocktail (or mocktail) at each, your tables reserved, and a digital route map. From €29,95 per person.
Is it really self-guided?
Yes — no guide and no group. Your route lands in your inbox the moment you book, and you walk it at your own pace. Most guests take 3–4 hours.
Which cities can I walk?
Amsterdam, Rotterdam, Utrecht and Groningen — with more cities on the way.
I don't drink alcohol — can I still come?
Absolutely. Every route works fully in mocktails. 0% alcohol, 100% of the night.
Is it good for a date night, birthday or group?
Perfect for all three. Most guests come in groups of two to eight; for 8+ we arrange a private group walk.
