    How group booking works

    No calendar puzzles — one short form, then a human takes over.

    1

    📝Send your enquiry

    Fill in the enquiry form with your city, date and group size — it takes two minutes. There's a company-name field for team outings, and room to tell us what you have in mind.

    2

    📩Get your quote — usually the same day

    On weekdays (10:00–18:00) we typically come back the same day with availability and your quote. Enquiring on a weekend? WhatsApp +31 20 210 1168 gets the fastest reply.

    3

    🗺️We build your route

    Once you confirm, we reserve tables for your whole group at all three bars and put your route together — personalised if you asked for it. Your digital map guide arrives by email before the walk.

    4

    🥂Walk it together

    Start at bar 1 at your agreed time, then take the night at your own pace — most groups take 3–4 relaxed hours, and you can stay for an extra round at the last bar. Curious about the format? See how every WOGO walk works →

    Group questions, answered

    How far ahead should we book a group walk?
    The earlier the better — the bars hold tables especially for your group, and weekend dates fill first. Send your enquiry as soon as you know your date; on weekdays we usually confirm availability the same day.
    Can we pick our own bars or route?
    Yes — personalised maps and routes are possible for group bookings. Tell us the vibe (or the bars) you have in mind in the enquiry and we'll build the route around it. Group routes are the one exception to WOGO's fixed bar 1 → 2 → 3 order.
    We're fewer than 6 — or more than 30. Now what?
    Fewer than 6? You don't need the group form — book any route instantly from €29,95 per person on the Rotterdam or Utrecht pages. More than 30? Contact us at info@wogoamsterdam.com or +31 20 210 1168 and we'll see what's possible.
    Which cities can we do a group walk in?
    Group walks currently run in Amsterdam and Rotterdam only. In Utrecht and Groningen you can book the regular routes for parties of up to 6.
