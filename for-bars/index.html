        The arrangement

        A fair, simple deal

        What we ask

        • A small signature menu: two cocktails and one alcohol-free drink, chosen by you. We design it in the WOGO style.
        • Table space for the group. Guests book within your own opening hours, so there are no slots to keep free.
        • A short briefing for your team. We write the one-page staff sheet, you put it behind the bar.

        What you get

        • A guaranteed fee for every guest who walks in, covering your cocktail cost. The same arrangement as every partner bar.
        • No commission and no onboarding fee, now or later.
        • A permanent place on the route, built so no two bars on it compete.
        • Your bar promoted through our website, socials and paid ads.

        Pricing is simple and we go through every line of it in the intro call, so you know exactly what you are signing.

        Before you ask

        Questions bars ask us

        Do we have to keep time slots free?
        No. Guests book the walk within your own opening hours, so there is nothing to block off and nothing to hold empty. They also walk at their own pace, so arrivals spread across the evening instead of landing all at once.
        What does it cost to join?
        Nothing to join. No commission and no onboarding fee. There is one simple per-guest arrangement that covers your cocktail cost, and we walk through it line by line in the intro call so you know exactly what you are signing.
        How many guests can we expect?
        It depends on the city, the route and the night of the week. We bring the real numbers for your city to the call rather than guessing here.
        Do we end up competing with the other bars on the route?
        No. A route is built so no two bars on it overlap, and you join permanently rather than as a temporary fill-in for one event.
        What do we have to serve?
        Three drinks you pick yourself: two cocktails and one alcohol-free. Guests choose one per stop, so there is no decision fatigue at the bar. We design the menu and you get it digitally, and many bars use it to introduce something exclusive.
        Are we tied into a contract?
        It is an ongoing partnership without a fixed term. Stopping is always possible, and we agree the notice period together in the intro call. Questions in the meantime go to info@wogoamsterdam.com.
