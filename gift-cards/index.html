        Gift card questions

        How does redeeming a gift card work?
        The gift card arrives by email with a unique code. When the lucky one books any walk on our site, they enter the code at checkout and the amount is deducted from the total. That's it — no account, no app.
        How long is a gift card valid?
        The exact validity is shown on the gift card itself when it arrives by email. Sitting on a card and not sure where you stand? Email us at info@wogoamsterdam.com and we'll check it for you.
        Can a card cover part of a bigger or group booking?
        Yes — a gift card doesn't have to cover the whole amount. Enter the code at checkout and pay the remainder as usual. Planning a walk for 7 or more? Head to group bookings and mention your code when you get in touch — we'll sort it together.
