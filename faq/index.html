    Booking & arrival

    In which cities can I do a WOGO cocktail walk?
    In four Dutch cities: Amsterdam, Rotterdam (three routes), Utrecht and Groningen — six routes in total, from €29,95 per person.
    Do I need to book in advance or can I show up?
    Yes — book ahead. We reserve all 3 tables for you, so there's no waiting and no walk-ins.
    When do I get my route map?
    Your digital Cocktail Walk Map Guide lands in your inbox right after booking, with your route from bar 1 to bar 2 to bar 3. No app needed — it works on your phone. Can't find it? Check your spam folder first, then email us at info@wogoamsterdam.com.
    Which bars will I visit?
    That's a surprise — the full line-up is revealed only after you book, in your map. We post sneak peeks on Instagram. Expect hand-picked spots you'd probably never have found on your own.
    How do I check in at each bar?
    Walk in, tell the bartender you booked with WOGO and give your full name — your table is waiting.
    What if a bar is unexpectedly closed?
    It's rare, but it happens. Contact the team at info@wogoamsterdam.com or +31 20 210 1168 and we'll arrange an alternative bar for you.

    What's included

    What exactly is in my ticket?
    Three things: a reserved table at 3 hand-picked bars, 3 drinks (at every bar you pick one of two signature WOGO cocktails, or the mocktail), and your digital Cocktail Walk Map Guide. One ticket covers one person.
    Can two people share one ticket?
    No — one ticket = one person's full walk. Everyone in your party needs their own ticket, so everyone gets their own reserved seat and their own three drinks.
    Is WOGO really self-guided?
    Completely. There's no guide, no tour group, no name badges — just you and your own people, at your own pace, with a route map on your phone. Curious what the night looks like? See how it works, step by step.
    Is food included?
    No — dinner isn't part of the ticket. But almost all stops serve snacks you can order; anything extra you settle directly with the bar.

    Timing & route

    How long does a cocktail walk take?
    Most guests take 3–4 relaxed hours. Only your start time at bar 1 is fixed — after that there's no time pressure at all. Stay as long as you like at each stop.
    How far apart are the bars?
    A 5–15 minute walk from each other — the stroll between stops is part of the experience.
    Can I change the route order?
    No — your tables are reserved in order, so the route always runs bar 1 → 2 → 3. The one exception: personalised routes for group bookings, which we plan around you.

    Drinks & dietary

    What drinks can I choose at each bar?
    Each bar has a special WOGO menu made for the walk: two signature cocktails and one mocktail. You pick one drink per bar — three in total.
    Can I do the whole walk alcohol-free?
    Yes — every route works as a full Mocktail Walk. Same bars, same reserved tables, an alcohol-free mocktail at every stop.

    Groups & gifts

    Can we book as a group?
    Absolutely — bachelorettes, birthdays and team outings are our speciality. Groups of 7 to 30 get their own arrangement, and we can even personalise your route. Start at our group bookings page.
    Can I give a cocktail walk as a gift?
    Yes — a WOGO gift card works for any route in any city and is delivered instantly by email. See gift cards.

    Policies & support

    Can I reschedule if my plans change?
    Get in touch at info@wogoamsterdam.com — we'll see what's possible.
    Do I need to be 18?
    Yes — Dutch law requires all guests to be 18+ to drink alcohol. Bring ID, just in case. Under-18s are welcome on the mocktail version when accompanied by an adult — contact us to arrange.
    How can I reach the WOGO team?
    On weekdays 10:00–18:00 by phone (+31 20 210 1168) or email (info@wogoamsterdam.com). On weekends we're reachable by WhatsApp, chat and email only — we'll get back to you as fast as we can.
