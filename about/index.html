    Our story

    Born in Amsterdam, during lockdown

    WOGO started in Amsterdam during the pandemic lockdowns, when the city's bars needed all the support they could get. Cocktail walks were our answer: a way to send guests to local bars, while giving residents a reason to rediscover the city they thought they already knew.

    That spirit never left. WOGO has grown into a platform that gives independent bars and spirit brands a stage — for cocktail newbies and enthusiasts alike — across Amsterdam, Rotterdam, Utrecht and Groningen, with more than 80,000 walks booked along the way.

    Our philosophy is simple: a night out should be like a well-crafted cocktail — balanced, complete, nothing missing. Good drinks, beautiful bars and, above all, good company.

    How we pick our bars

    Every bar on a WOGO route is hand-picked by our team in Amsterdam. This is what a bar has to get right before it earns a spot on a route.

    💎

    Hidden gems, hand-picked

    We look for independent bars you'd probably never find on your own — places with real character, from speakeasies to skybars. Every one is chosen by hand, never from a list.

    🚶

    Walkable by design

    Routes are planned so the bars sit an optimal 5–15 minutes' walk apart — far enough to feel the city change between stops, close enough that nobody's feet complain.

    🪑

    Your table is pre-arranged

    The moment you book, we arrange your table at all three bars. You never queue and never hover for a seat — you walk in and sit down.

    🤝

    Staff who expect you

    Bar staff are briefed on WOGO arrivals and pour from a special WOGO menu. Give your name at the bar and the night simply starts.
