    The route

    Three bars, one unforgettable night

    Looking for the best cocktail bars in Witte de With, Rotterdam? This self-guided bar crawl takes you to three of them in one night — specially curated for you, with a table reserved and a signature cocktail waiting at every stop.

    1 · A vibrant new hotspot

    Begin your evening in Witte de With's newest hotspot — special cocktails, big energy, and the perfect start on Rotterdam's most famous bar street.

    2 · A local gem everyone knows

    Your next venue is just one hop away: a local gem every Rotterdammer knows. Walk in, give your name — your table is already waiting.

    3 · A Tokyo-style bar

    Continue the night in a unique Tokyo-style bar and get surprised by drinks you won't find anywhere else in the city.

    Which bars exactly? That stays a surprise until you've booked — we post sneak peeks on our socials.
